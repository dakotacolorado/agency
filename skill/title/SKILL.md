# Title

Every agent is named for its role and id, which says nothing about its work. Your
title is what the person reads first on your card and your page, so the list
of agents reads as what is going on.

## Calling it

Run `"$CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR/skills/title/bin/title"`:

```sh
title 'Short status headline'
title 'agency on SQLite'
```

At most 40 characters: what you are there to do, in a few words. It is not
a status. It stays as the work moves, across sessions.

## When

Once, early in your first session, as soon as you know your work. If it
answers 409 you already have one, from an earlier session or from the
person; keep it. Only the person changes it after that.
